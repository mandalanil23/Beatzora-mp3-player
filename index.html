Privacy Policy — how Beatzora handles information and permissions while you enjoy your music.

Free for lifetime No advertisements Offline music playback

1. Introduction

Welcome to Beatzora MP3 Player (“Beatzora”, “we”, “our”, or “the app”). Beatzora is a free Android music player designed to play audio files stored on your device. This Privacy Policy explains how the app accesses and uses information when you use its features.

Effective date: October 11, 2026

2. Information We Collect

Beatzora is designed for local music playback and does not require you to create an account.

  • Personal information: Beatzora does not ask you to provide your name, phone number, or email address to play music.
  • Audio files and song details: The app accesses music files and related details, such as song title, artist, album, or duration, when needed to display and play your local music.
  • Favorites and app settings: The app may save your favorite-song selections and playback or sound settings locally on your device, depending on the app implementation.

We do not intentionally upload your music library or audio files to our servers.

3. How Beatzora Uses Music Access

Access to audio files is used only to support the app's music-player functions, including:

  • Finding and listing supported audio files stored on your device.
  • Playing, pausing, and controlling music playback.
  • Marking songs as favorites.
  • Deleting a song when you choose the delete option and approve any Android system confirmation that may be required.
  • Setting a selected song as a ringtone when supported by your Android version and the required permission or system confirmation is provided.
  • Providing Extra Bass and volume-control features where supported by the device.

4. Permissions

Depending on your Android version and the features you use, Beatzora may request access to audio files or music, and may require permission or system approval to change a ringtone.

Permissions are used to provide the feature that requires them. You can review or change app permissions in your Android device settings. If you deny a permission, the related feature may not work.

Beatzora does not need microphone access to play music files. If a version of the app requests microphone access for another feature, this policy should be updated to explain that feature before release.

5. Ads, Payments, and Internet Use

Beatzora is intended to be free for lifetime use and does not display advertisements. The app does not charge a subscription or payment for its standard music-player features.

Core music playback is designed to use audio files stored locally on your device and does not require online music streaming. Your app build and any included software development kits should be checked to confirm whether any network communication occurs for other purposes, such as updates, diagnostics, or crash reports.

6. Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell your personal information. Beatzora is not designed to send your local music files to other people or to our servers.

If a future or current version includes third-party analytics, crash-reporting, or other software libraries, those providers may process technical information under their own privacy policies. This section must be revised to identify any such services actually included in the released app.

7. Data Storage, Retention, and Deletion

Music files remain in the storage location where you saved them unless you choose to delete a file using the app or another device feature. Deleting a song may permanently remove that file, depending on the Android version and the confirmation flow, so please check the selected file before confirming deletion.

Favorites and settings may be stored locally by the app. Uninstalling Beatzora may remove app-specific local data, but it does not necessarily remove music files stored in shared device storage. Any information handled by third-party services may be retained according to their policies.

8. Security

We aim to limit access to information to what is needed for the app's features. No software or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Keep your device and Android software up to date and use your device's security features.

9. Children's Privacy

Beatzora does not knowingly request personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been provided to us in connection with the app, please contact us using the details below. If the app is directed to children or is enrolled in a family-focused program, additional requirements may apply and this policy must be adjusted accordingly.

10. Your Choices

  • Manage audio and other permissions through Android Settings.
  • Choose which songs to mark as favorites.
  • Use the delete feature carefully and confirm only when you intend to delete the selected file.
  • Uninstall the app whenever you no longer wish to use it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app's features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The revised policy will be published at the same public URL with an updated effective date.
